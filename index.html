Smart Bank AI Platform

Flask · XGBoost · SHAP · KMeans
Practice / Training Project (NTI Internship)

Banks answer the same four questions every day — who to lend to, who to trust, where a currency is heading, who a client really is — usually manually, or across disconnected tools.

I built a full-stack platform where every decision is backed by a trained model, not static rules: a Flask API with a model registry that loads all four pipelines at startup, role-based auth, and one endpoint per model — behind separate client and manager portals.

  • Loan approval — XGBoost, best of 5 benchmarked models · 93.1% accuracy, 0.978 ROC-AUC, with an instant SHAP-explained decision on the client's screen.
  • Fraud detection — evaluated on PR-AUC (0.96) since fraud is <0.6% of transactions; a flag never moves money silently — it lands in the manager's review queue with probability and reasoning attached.
  • Currency forecasting — regression on lag & moving-average features for next-day forecasts.
  • Customer segmentation — KMeans clustering grouping clients into actionable segments.

AutoWorth AI — Used Car Price & Deal Advisor

Streamlit · XGBoost · 100K rows · 5 models
Practice / Training Project (WE Traineeship)

Buying a used car comes down to one question: is this a fair price, or am I overpaying?

AutoWorth AI predicts a car's fair market value and tells buyers whether the asking price is a good deal. Trained on a 100,000-row UK used-car dataset, the pipeline was built from scratch — acquisition, cleaning, EDA, feature engineering and tuning — benchmarking five models on R², MAE and RMSE. A Smart Deal Advisor then compares predicted value to the asking price, from Great Deal to Overpriced. Deployed as an interactive Streamlit app.

  • Full pipeline from scratch — cleaning → EDA → feature engineering → hyperparameter tuning, no shortcuts.
  • 5 models benchmarked — Linear Regression through XGBoost, selected on metrics, not gut feeling.
  • Smart Deal Advisor — predicted market value vs. asking price, classified instantly for the buyer.
